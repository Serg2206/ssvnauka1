
# 🎬 Best in Surgery Video Integration

## Overview

Your SSV Nauka platform now includes **18 professional surgical videos** from Best in Surgery - an educational project by Professor K.V. Puchkov featuring laparoscopic operations from world-leading surgeons.

## What is Best in Surgery?

Best in Surgery (www.best-in-surgery.ru) is a Russian educational platform providing high-quality laparoscopic surgical videos for medical professionals. Videos are available on **YouTube** and **Rutube** for free educational access.
