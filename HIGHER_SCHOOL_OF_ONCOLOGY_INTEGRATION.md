
# 🎓 Higher School of Oncology - CT Integration

## Overview

Your SSV Nauka platform now includes **8 educational modules** from **Higher School of Oncology** (Высшая школа онкологии) - a leading Russian oncology education center providing advanced training for oncologists in CT interpretation.

## What is Higher School of Oncology?

Higher School of Oncology (higherschoolofoncology.ru) is a Russian educational platform providing professional training for oncologists and medical professionals. Their **"CT in Oncology" intensive** teaches radiological diagnostics skills essential for modern oncology practice.
